About TET Test Hub

We built TET Test Hub for one reason: to give TET aspirants full-length practice that feels like the real exam.

Each mock test follows the real TET pattern: 150 questions from Child Development & Pedagogy, Language-1 (English), Language-2 (Hindi), Mathematics and Environmental Studies. You get 150 minutes, a live timer, a question palette and a section-wise result as soon as you submit. You can read every question in English or Hindi.

It's simple: Test 01 is free in every series. To unlock the other 24 tests, log in and pay ₹199 on the site. They unlock on your account straight away, with no waiting.
