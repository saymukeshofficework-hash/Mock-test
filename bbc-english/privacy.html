Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026.

What we store

This learning platform stores your name, goal, XP, streak, quiz scores and lesson progress locally in your browser's storage (localStorage). This data stays on your device — it is not uploaded to any server, because this app currently has no backend server.

Microphone access

The Speaking Lab may ask for microphone permission so you can record and play back your own voice for self-practice. Recordings stay on your device only, are not uploaded anywhere, and no automated speech or pronunciation analysis is performed on them.

Contact form

The "Book a Free Demo" form opens WhatsApp or your email app with your details pre-filled, so the information is sent directly through the service you choose — this app does not store or transmit form data itself.

Clearing your data

You can clear all locally stored progress at any time by clearing your browser's site data for this app, or using your browser's private/incognito mode.

Contact us

For any privacy questions, please reach out via the contact details on our Contact page.
