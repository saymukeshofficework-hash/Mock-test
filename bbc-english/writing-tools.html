For School Students

Student Writing Tools

Formats and real examples for notice writing, letter writing, paragraph writing and speech preparation — including Independence Day and Republic Day speeches.

Format: School name (top) · Date · Word "NOTICE" (centred) · Underlined heading naming the topic · Body in 50–80 words covering what/when/where/who · Issued by, with designation.
Formal letter format: Receiver's designation and address · Subject line · Salutation ("Respected Sir/Madam") · Body in 3 short paragraphs · Polite closing ("Yours obediently/faithfully") · Your name.

Informal letter format: Salutation ("Dear ___") · Friendly, conversational body · Warm closing ("Your friend/loving daughter") · Your name.
Format: Start with a clear topic sentence · 3–4 supporting sentences with examples or reasons · End with a concluding thought · Keep it to one solid paragraph (100–150 words), no headings or bullet points.
Format: Greeting/address to the audience · An opening line about the occasion · 2–3 main points or a short story · A closing call-to-action or thought · "Thank you". Replace bracketed placeholders like [Your School Name] with your own details.
