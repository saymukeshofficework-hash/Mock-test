## Component Deep Dive: Why These Parts?

---

### 1. Processor: AMD Ryzen 5 5600 (~₹9,500)

The 6-core, 12-thread **AMD Ryzen 5 5600** remains the uncontested king of budget PC gaming in India.

* **Why Not Intel Core i5-12400F?** The i5-12400F is an excellent alternative at roughly ₹8,800. However, matching budget Intel motherboards (H610 chipset) severely limit PCIe lanes and lack memory overclocking (XMP can be finicky), while affordable B550 motherboards for the Ryzen 5 5600 support full **PCIe 4.0**, faster memory profiles, and future CPU drops (such as the Ryzen 7 5700X3D).
* **The Secret Weapon:** The Ryzen 5 5600 packs a massive **32MB of L3 GameCache**, which dramatically reduces frame-time spikes and eliminates stuttering in CPU-intensive multiplayer games like *Valorant* and *Battlefield*.
* **Stock Cooler Included:** It comes bundled with the AMD Wraith Stealth cooler in the box, saving you ₹1,500 on an aftermarket CPU cooler.

---

### 2. Graphics Card: AMD Radeon RX 6600 8GB GDDR6 (~₹18,500)

Allocating nearly 38% of your total ₹50,000 budget to the GPU is the golden rule of PC gaming. The **AMD Radeon RX 6600 8GB** (from reliable board partners like Sapphire Pulse, PowerColor Fighter, or ASRock Challenger) completely obliterates the competition at this price point.

#### The RTX 3050 Trap to Avoid:

NVIDIA sells a cut-down RTX 3050 6GB for around ₹16,500. **Do not buy it.** That card features a crippled 96-bit memory bus and delivers nearly **40% lower performance** than the RX 6600.

* The RX 6600 features a full **8GB of GDDR6 VRAM**, a 128-bit memory bus, and 32MB of on-die Infinity Cache.
* It outpaces the older GTX 1660 Super, RTX 2060, and RTX 3050 easily, delivering native 60+ FPS in virtually every modern title at 1080p.

---

### 3. Motherboard: MSI B550M PRO-VDH WiFi (~₹7,200)

Never cheap out on the motherboard with a bottom-barrel A320 or A520 board just to save ₹1,500.

* **Full PCIe 4.0 Support:** Ensures your RX 6600 GPU runs at full PCIe 4.0 x8 bandwidth and your NVMe SSD achieves maximum read speeds.
* **Dual M.2 Slots & VRM Heatsinks:** Features extended aluminum heatsinks over the power delivery stages, preventing thermal throttling during sustained workloads.
* **Built-in Wi-Fi & Bluetooth:** Saves you the extra ₹1,200 cost of buying external USB Wi-Fi dongles and lets you connect wireless gamepads directly.

---

### 4. Memory (RAM): 16GB (2x8GB) DDR4 3200MHz CL16 (~₹3,200)

* **Always Run Dual-Channel:** Never buy a single 16GB stick. Running two 8GB sticks in dual-channel configuration doubles the memory bus bandwidth, boosting minimum frame rates in games by 15% to 25%.
* **Speed & Timings:** 3200MHz CL16 is the sweet spot for the Ryzen 5000 series memory controller, perfectly aligning with the processor's Infinity Fabric clock (1:1 ratio).

---

### 5. Storage: 1TB Western Digital Blue SN580 Gen4 NVMe SSD (~₹5,200)

Modern games are gargantuan: *Call of Duty* requires 120GB+, *GTA V* takes 110GB, and *Forza Horizon 5* demands 130GB. A 512GB SSD will leave you with room for only two games after installing Windows 11.

* The **WD Blue SN580 1TB** utilizes high-speed PCIe Gen4 x4 technology, delivering blazing **4,150 MB/s read speeds**. Game levels load in seconds, texture pop-in is eliminated, and daily Windows boot times drop to under 8 seconds.

---

### 6. Power Supply (PSU): Deepcool PK550D / Corsair CV550 80+ Bronze (~₹3,400)

The power supply is the heart of your computer. A cheap, uncertified power supply can fry your entire system during Indian voltage fluctuations.

* The **Deepcool PK550D** (or Corsair CV550) is an **80-Plus Bronze certified** power supply delivering 550 watts of clean continuous power.
* This build consumes approximately 260W to 280W under full gaming load, meaning a 550W PSU operates right in its peak 50% efficiency curve, running silent and cool while leaving ample headroom for future GPU upgrades.

---

### 7. Cabinet: Ant Esports ICE-112 / Galax Revolution 05 (~₹2,800)

Do not buy a sealed glass box. Indian summer ambient temperatures demand airflow.

* Look for an ATX/M-ATX cabinet with a **full perforated mesh front panel**.
* Cabinets in this ₹2,800 price bracket come with **four pre-installed 120mm auto-RGB cooling fans** (three front intake fans, one rear exhaust fan), ensuring your CPU and GPU stay below 70°C without requiring expensive liquid cooling.

1. **Don't Buy Windows at Retail Price:** You do not need to spend ₹9,000 on a retail Windows 11 license. You can download the official Windows 11 installation media directly from Microsoft's website for free onto an 8GB pen drive and activate it using student licenses or OEM digital keys.
2. **Enable XMP/DOCP in BIOS:** After assembling your PC, your RAM will default to 2133MHz. Restart your PC, press `Del` to enter the BIOS, and toggle on **DOCP (Direct Over Clock Profile)** to unlock your full 3200MHz RAM speed.
3. **Connect Display Cable to GPU, Not Motherboard:** The most common first-time builder mistake is plugging the HDMI/DisplayPort cable into the motherboard I/O. The Ryzen 5 5600 has no integrated graphics; always plug your monitor cable directly into the horizontal ports on the **Radeon RX 6600 graphics card**.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. Can this ₹50,000 PC handle 1440p (2K) gaming?

Yes, but with caveats. The RX 6600 is primarily a 1080p card. It can run esports games (*Valorant*, *Rocket League*) at 1440p above 144 FPS easily. For demanding AAA titles like *Cyberpunk 2077* or *Red Dead Redemption 2*, playing at 1440p requires turning on AMD FSR (FidelityFX Super Resolution) on the Quality preset and adjusting graphics to medium settings.

### 2. Is this PC good for video editing and programming?

Yes. The 6-core/12-thread Ryzen 5 5600 paired with 16GB RAM and a fast NVMe SSD handles 4K timeline scrubbing in Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve smoothly. For programmers, it compiles codebases rapidly and runs multiple Docker containers and Android emulators without stutter.

### 3. Can I upgrade this PC in the future?

Yes. The AM4 platform allows you to drop in a Ryzen 7 5700X3D (one of the fastest gaming CPUs on the market) down the line without changing your motherboard or RAM. The 550W power supply also has enough wattage to support graphics cards up to an RTX 4060 or RX 7600 XT in the future.

### 4. Is the AMD Wraith Stealth stock cooler enough for the Ryzen 5 5600?

Yes, for normal gaming and productivity, the bundled AMD stock cooler keeps CPU temperatures between 68°C and 76°C in an airflow-focused mesh cabinet. If you live in an area with extreme 42°C+ summer ambient temperatures, adding a budget ₹1,400 single-tower air cooler (like the Deepcool AG400) will keep temperatures under 65°C under all loads.

### 5. Why choose AMD RX 6600 over NVIDIA RTX 3050?

The RTX 3050 6GB is vastly inferior. The RX 6600 features 8GB VRAM, higher memory bandwidth (128-bit vs 96-bit), and delivers roughly 35% to 45% higher gaming frame rates for almost the exact same price.
