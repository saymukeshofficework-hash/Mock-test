## What "AI Writing Tool" Actually Covers

The category spans very different products: general-purpose AI assistants (like ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini) that can draft or edit text among many other tasks, and purpose-built writing tools focused specifically on grammar, tone, or long-form drafting. They aren't interchangeable, and the right choice depends on the task.

## A Framework for Evaluating One

1. **What's the actual task?** Drafting from scratch, editing existing text, summarizing, or brainstorming each stress-test a tool differently.
2. **How much editing control do you get?** Some tools let you steer tone and structure closely; others are closer to a black box.
3. **How does it handle facts?** AI writing tools can produce confident-sounding but incorrect statements. Any factual claim needs independent verification before publishing.
4. **Does it fit your existing workflow?** A tool that requires switching context constantly often costs more time than it saves.

## The Bottom Line

Treat AI writing output as a draft, not a finished product. The tools are genuinely useful for speed and iteration — but factual accuracy and final judgment remain the writer's responsibility.
