General Instructions:

  1. Total duration of examination is 150 minutes (2 Hours 30 Minutes).
  2. The test has 150 questions across 5 sections — Child Development & Pedagogy, Language-1 (English), Language-2 (Hindi), Mathematics and Environmental Studies — 30 questions each, 1 mark per question, no negative marking, total 150 marks.
  3. The clock will be set at the server. The countdown timer will display the remaining time. When the timer reaches zero, the examination will end and auto-submit.
  4. The Question Palette on the right will show the status of each question using the symbols below:
1 You have not visited the question yet.

2 You have not answered the question.

3 You have answered the question.

4 You have marked the question for review.

  1. The "Mark for Review" status simply means you would like to look at that question again. If a question is answered and marked for review, your answer for that question will still be considered in evaluation, unless changed.
  2. To answer a question, select an option and click "Save & Next".
  3. To deselect a chosen answer, click "Clear Response".
  4. To change your answer, select another option and click "Save & Next".
  5. Sections in this test will be displayed as tabs at the top. Questions in a section can be viewed by clicking the section name.
  6. You can navigate between sections and questions anytime during the test, subject to the time constraint.
