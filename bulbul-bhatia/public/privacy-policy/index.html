Astro by Bulbul Bhatia is a Vedic astrology (Jyotish), Kundali and Tarot app. This page explains what information the app uses and why.

Information the app handles

All of the following is optional and is provided by you:

  • Name – used for birth chart profiles and the Contact Us form.
  • Email address – if you sign in with Google, or if you write it in the Contact Us form.
  • Phone number – only if you enter it in the Contact Us / consultation form.
  • Birth date, birth time and birth city – used to calculate planetary positions, Kundali charts, Dashas and reports.

Saved profiles, tarot readings and journal notes are created by you inside the app.

How the information is used

  • To provide app features: charts, Dasha timelines, calculators, matching, Tarot readings and PDF reports.
  • To manage sign-in (Google Sign-In) and your account.
  • To receive and reply to your consultation or contact requests.

We do not use your information for advertising. The app contains no ads.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it with third parties for their own use. Data you submit is stored with Google Firebase services that power the app, and is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).

Your choices and data deletion

You can delete saved profiles, readings and inquiries inside the app. You can also ask us to delete your data by emailing saymukeshofficework@gmail.com, or by following the steps on our data deletion page.

Children

The app is intended for users aged 18 and over and is not directed to children.

Disclaimer

Astrological calculations, Dasha timelines, Yogas, Doshas, remedies and Tarot readings are for cultural study, spiritual reflection and personal insight. They do not replace professional medical, legal, psychological or financial advice.
