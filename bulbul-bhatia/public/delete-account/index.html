Delete Your Account and Data

This page is for users of the Astro by Bulbul Bhatia Android app (developer: Mukesh Singh Dahiya).

Option 1: Delete inside the app

You can delete saved birth profiles, tarot readings and journal entries, and inquiries you submitted, directly from the app.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

  1. Send an email to saymukeshofficework@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Astro by Bulbul Bhatia data.
  2. Write from the email address you used in the app (the Google account email, or the email you entered in the Contact Us form).
  3. Say whether you want all your data deleted, or only some of it (for example, only the consultation inquiries).

You can also use the Contact Us form inside the app and ask for deletion there.

What gets deleted

  • Your name, email address and phone number that you entered in the app or the Contact Us form.
  • Birth date, birth time and birth city, and the saved profiles, readings and notes linked to them.
  • Your consultation inquiries and any notes we hold about them.

If we must keep any record for a legal reason, we will tell you what it is and why when we reply.
