2025: Major Highlights

January: Maha Kumbh 2025 began in Prayagraj on 13 January. Padma Awards 2025 were announced on 25 January: 139 awards. ISRO launched NVS-02 on 29 January using GSLV-F15.
February: Union Budget 2025–26 was presented on 1 February. Agriculture, MSMEs, investment and exports were identified as four engines of development.

Budget and Economy

Union Budget 2025–26

  • Presented on 1 February 2025 by Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman.
  • Four engines of development: Agriculture, MSMEs, Investment and Exports.
  • Prime Minister Dhan-Dhaanya Krishi Yojana proposed coverage of 100 low agricultural-productivity districts, with an intended reach of about 1.7 crore farmers.
  • Mission for Aatmanirbharta in Pulses focused on Tur, Urad and Masoor.
  • Under the new tax regime, no income tax was payable up to ₹12 lakh of normal income; for salaried taxpayers, the effective threshold was ₹12.75 lakh due to the ₹75,000 standard deduction. Special-rate income may be treated differently.
  • Fiscal-deficit target for FY 2025–26: 4.4% of GDP.

Science, Space and Technology

ISRO and India's Space Programme

  • GSLV-F15 / NVS-02: Launched on 29 January 2025; it marked ISRO's 100th launch from Sriharikota.
  • SpaDeX: In 2025, India demonstrated autonomous docking and undocking of satellites, becoming the fourth country to demonstrate space-docking capability.
  • Axiom-4: Launched on 25 June 2025. Group Captain Shubhanshu Shukla became the first Indian to visit the ISS. During an approximately 18-day mission he carried out seven microgravity experiments and returned to Earth on 15 July.
  • NISAR: A joint NASA–ISRO Earth-observation mission, launched on 30 July 2025 aboard GSLV-F16.

Awards, Honours and Literature

Padma Awards 2025

  • 139 awards in total: 7 Padma Vibhushan, 19 Padma Bhushan and 113 Padma Shri.
  • The list included 23 women, 10 awardees from foreign/NRI/PIO/OCI categories and 13 posthumous awards.
  • Padma Awards are generally announced on the eve of Republic Day.

58th Jnanpith Award

The 58th Jnanpith Award for the year 2024 was conferred on Gulzar and Jagadguru Rambhadracharya. President Droupadi Murmu presented the honour on 16 May 2025.

Sports 2025

India women's cricket team with the World Cup trophy; Prime Minister's Office / GODL-India, 5 November 2025.

ICC Men's Champions Trophy 2025

  • Final: India vs New Zealand, 9 March 2025, Dubai.
  • India defeated New Zealand by four wickets.
  • India won the Champions Trophy for the third time and remained unbeaten in the tournament.
  • Rohit Sharma scored 76 in the final.

ICC Women's Cricket World Cup 2025

  • Final: India vs South Africa, 2 November 2025, Navi Mumbai.
  • India defeated South Africa by 52 runs to win its first-ever Women's ODI World Cup.
  • Shafali Verma scored 87; Deepti Sharma scored 58 and took five wickets.

Sports Governance

The National Sports Governance Act, 2025 was notified on 18 August 2025. A notification issued on 31 December 2025 appointed 1 January 2026 as the commencement date for selected provisions.

Environment, Geography and UNESCO

  • ‘Maratha Military Landscapes of India’ was inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage List on 11 July 2025. It became India's 44th World Heritage property and includes 12 forts.
  • India's 2025 year-end environment review reported 11 new Ramsar sites added during 2025, taking the total to 96 during the year. Always note the reference date when using changing counts.
  • Indore in Madhya Pradesh and Udaipur became India's first Ramsar-accredited Wetland Cities in 2025.
  • Important environmental topics: biodiversity, wetlands, Ramsar Convention, wildlife conservation, climate change and the National Clean Air Programme.

Madhya Pradesh Focus

  • Dr Mohan Yadav was the Chief Minister of Madhya Pradesh in 2025.
  • Ujjain is a major historic and religious city in Madhya Pradesh and the home city of Chief Minister Dr Mohan Yadav.
  • For MP examinations, separately revise rivers, national parks, tiger reserves, tribes, folk dances, major schemes, budget, appointments and tourism sites.
This edition avoids adding state-specific annual figures where a clear official 2025 source was not available for verification.

National Affairs and Governance

  • According to the Ministry of Parliamentary Affairs year-end review, 39 Bills were passed by both Houses of Parliament during 2025.
  • The National Sports Governance Act, 2025 aims to promote transparency, accountability and good governance in sports administration.
  • National programmes and parliamentary discussions marked the 150th anniversary of the national song ‘Vande Mataram’ in 2025.
  • For government schemes, revise the ministry, objective, target beneficiaries, funding and launch/amendment year.

International Affairs

Useful exam topics include headquarters, membership and 2025 developments of UNESCO, the United Nations, WHO, IMF, World Bank, WTO, G20, BRICS, SCO and ASEAN. Study major global awards, climate meetings, sports events and agreements with their dates and locations.
