Month-wise Major Events

January: 12 January: ISRO's launch record lists the PSLV-C62 / EOS-N1 mission.
February: 1 February: Union Budget 2026–27 presented. 6 February: India defeated England by 100 runs to win the ICC Under-19 Men's World Cup for a record sixth time.
March: 8 March: India defeated New Zealand by 96 runs to win the ICC Men's T20 World Cup 2026. India became the first team to defend the title and won it for a third time.
May: 25 May: The President's Secretariat issued the Padma Awards 2026 notification. Main categories: Padma Vibhushan, Padma Bhushan and Padma Shri.
July: 4 July: India–Israel Bilateral Investment Agreement entered into force. 15 July: India–UK CETA and the Agreement on Social Security entered into force.
August: The MSME Development (Amendment) Bill, 2026 was passed by Parliament. The GOBARdhan National Circular Bioenergy Scheme was approved.
September: 4 September: ISRO's list records GSLV-F17 / EOS-05. 13 September: India defeated Sri Lanka to win a record-extending eighth Women's Asia Cup cricket title.
October: 3 October: India defeated Pakistan to win gold in men's cricket at the Asian Games 2026. 30 October: ISRO's list records GSLV-F18 / NVS-03.
November (through 14 Nov): 8 November: Anuj Shrivatri became India's 100th and Madhya Pradesh's first chess Grandmaster. 11 November: Jabalpur featured among the top three Swachh Shehar in the million-plus population category.

Budget and Economy

Union Budget 2026–27

  • Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman presented the Union Budget on 1 February 2026.
  • The Budget was guided by three Kartavyas: accelerating economic growth; building people's capacity; and ensuring access to opportunities across regions and communities.
  • Budget Estimates: non-debt receipts of about ₹36.5 lakh crore and total expenditure of about ₹53.5 lakh crore.
  • PRS analysis places the fiscal-deficit target for 2026–27 at 4.3% of GDP.
These are budget estimates; actual receipts and expenditure may differ.

Sports

  • Men's T20 World Cup: India beat New Zealand by 96 runs; third title and first team to win consecutive editions.
  • U19 Men's World Cup: India beat England by 100 runs to win a record sixth title.
  • Women's Asia Cup: India beat Sri Lanka to win an eighth title.
  • Asian Games: India beat Pakistan in the men's cricket final to win gold.

Madhya Pradesh Special

On 8 November 2026, Anuj Shrivatri won the 7th ASEAN Plus Individual Open Chess Championship in the Philippines, becoming India's 100th and Madhya Pradesh's first Grandmaster.

In the Swachh Survekshan 2025–26 results announced on 11 November 2026, Jabalpur featured among the top three clean cities in the million-plus population category.
