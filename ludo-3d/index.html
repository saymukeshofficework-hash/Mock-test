How to Play

Objective

Get all four of your tokens from your base, around the board, and into your final home before your opponents.

How to Move

Roll the dice, then tap a glowing token to move it exactly that many spaces along the path.

Rolling a Six

A six lets you bring a new token out of your base — and always earns you another roll.

Capture

Land on an opponent's token to send it straight back to their base — unless it's sitting on a safe cell.

Safe cell Start cell

Winning

The first player to bring all four tokens home wins the game!

About Ludo 3D

A premium, fully client-side 3D take on the classic Ludo board game — built with Three.js. No account, no server: just local pass-and-play with 2 to 4 players on one device.
